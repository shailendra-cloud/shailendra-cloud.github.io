About

Shailendra Tiwari is an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science & Engineering at South Asian University, New Delhi. His research is in computational imaging and computer vision: recovering faithful images from incomplete, noisy or degraded measurements, and building learning systems that stay reliable on real-world data.

His doctoral work focused on statistical and regularised reconstruction for CT, PET and SPECT, a thread that continues in his recent work on training-free sparse-view CT reconstruction and fractional-order spectral regularisation for MLEM. Alongside it he works on underwater scene restoration and polarization estimation, EEG- and ECG-based driver monitoring and cognitive-load assessment, sEMG gesture recognition, and auditing medical-imaging benchmarks for patient-level data leakage.

He received his Ph.D. in Computer Science & Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology (BHU), Varanasi, and his M.E. in Computer Engineering from Punjab Engineering College, Chandigarh, and was a visiting research fellow (post-doctoral) in the School of Computer Science & Statistics at Trinity College Dublin. Before joining SAU he was a faculty member in Computer Science & Engineering at Thapar Institute of Engineering & Technology, Patiala, and earlier at Lovely Professional University, where he designed courses on cyber-crime, computer forensics and cyber law.

Research

Medical image reconstruction

Low-dose and sparse-view CT, PET and SPECT: statistical (MLEM/OSEM) and regularised iterative reconstruction with diffusion, dictionary-learning and fractional-order spectral priors.

  • CT
  • PET / SPECT
  • Inverse problems

Image restoration & vision

Underwater scene restoration and polarization estimation, medical image retrieval, biometrics, and detection and tracking in video.

  • Underwater imaging
  • Retrieval
  • Biometrics

Biomedical signals

EEG–ECG fusion for driver cognitive load and drowsiness, sEMG gesture recognition, and inner-speech EEG decoding.

  • EEG
  • ECG
  • sEMG

Reliable machine learning

Subject-wise (leave-one-subject-out) evaluation, patient-level leakage auditing, and reproducibility of published models.

  • LOSO
  • Data leakage
  • Reproducibility

Graphics, animation & simulation
