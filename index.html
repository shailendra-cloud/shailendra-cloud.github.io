About

Shailendra Tiwari is an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science & Engineering at Thapar Institute of Engineering & Technology, Patiala, where he has taught since 2016. His primary research area is computer vision and medical image reconstruction: non-invasive techniques that produce tomographic images of the anatomy and function of organs from projection data.

His doctoral work at IIT (BHU) Varanasi developed efficient frameworks for medical image reconstruction, and that thread continues in his recent work on low-dose and training-free sparse-view CT. Alongside it he works on underwater scene restoration, multimodal learning, precision agriculture, and EEG- and ECG-based driver monitoring, and he audits medical-imaging benchmarks for patient-level data leakage.

In 2017–18 he was an institution-sponsored post-doctoral fellow in computer animation and gaming at Trinity College Dublin, an experience that shaped the Computer Vision and 3D Modelling & Animation courses he coordinates at Thapar. He has supervised doctoral theses on medical image reconstruction, multimodal retrieval and underwater vision, and is co-PI on a DST-FIST grant in graphics, vision and visualization.

Where the work is used

Reconstruction methods make CT safer by recovering diagnostic images from fewer or weaker X-ray projections. The same computer-vision toolkit carries over to crops, roads, factories and cities, which is why his student projects range from crop monitoring to driver safety.
