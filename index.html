About

Shailendra Tiwari is an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science & Engineering at Thapar Institute of Engineering & Technology, Patiala. His research is in computational imaging and computer vision: recovering faithful images from incomplete, noisy or degraded measurements, and building learning systems that stay reliable on real-world data.

His doctoral work focused on statistical and regularised reconstruction for CT, PET and SPECT, a thread that continues in his recent work on training-free sparse-view CT reconstruction and fractional-order spectral regularisation for MLEM. Alongside it he works on underwater scene restoration and polarization estimation, EEG- and ECG-based driver monitoring and cognitive-load assessment, sEMG gesture recognition, and auditing medical-imaging benchmarks for patient-level data leakage.

He received his Ph.D. in Computer Science & Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology (BHU), Varanasi, and his M.E. in Computer Engineering from Punjab Engineering College, Chandigarh, and was a visiting research fellow (post-doctoral) in the School of Computer Science & Statistics at Trinity College Dublin. Earlier he taught at Lovely Professional University, where he designed courses on cyber-crime, computer forensics and cyber law.

Research

The common thread is recovering reliable information from imperfect measurements: undersampled CT data, degraded underwater images and noisy physiological signals.

Medical image reconstruction

CT, PET and SPECT, inverse problems

Low-dose and sparse-view CT, PET and SPECT. Statistical (MLEM, OSEM) and regularised iterative reconstruction with diffusion, dictionary-learning and fractional-order spectral priors, including training-free methods that need no matched training data.

Image restoration and vision

Underwater imaging, retrieval, biometrics

Underwater scene restoration and single-image polarization estimation, medical image retrieval, biometric recognition, and detection and tracking in video.

Biomedical signals

EEG, ECG, sEMG

Attention-based EEG–ECG fusion for driver cognitive load and drowsiness, sEMG gesture recognition, and inner-speech EEG decoding.

Reliable machine learning

Data leakage, subject-wise evaluation, reproducibility

Leave-one-subject-out evaluation, auditing benchmarks for patient-level leakage, and checking whether published models reproduce.

Graphics and simulation

Unity, Blender, OpenGL

Physically based modelling, 3D modelling and animation, and simulation in game engines.
